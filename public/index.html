📍 Serving Jaipur with love

Hello, I'm Dr. Sparsh! 👋

Born and raised in Jaipur, I pursued my passion for dentistry at the prestigious SDM College of Dental Sciences, Dharwad — affiliated with RGUHS, Karnataka. After completing my BDS, I gained invaluable clinical experience across Hubli, Dharwad, Bangalore, and the broader dental landscape of Karnataka.

I went on to serve as Ex-Clinic Head at one of India's largest dental chains, where I led a team of specialists and managed high patient volumes — honing skills in both clinical excellence and patient care management.

Eventually, my heart brought me back home to serve my hometown Jaipur — specifically Jagatpura, where I founded LOL Dental. My mission is simple: bring world-class, painless dental care to every family in my neighbourhood, with a smile.
