Surgical

Dental Implants

The gold standard for tooth replacement. Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed in your jawbone to provide a permanent, stable foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

  • Single tooth implants
  • Full arch implant solutions
  • Implant-supported crowns & bridges
  • Bone grafting if required
  • 3D planning for precision placement

Orthodontics

Metal Braces

The tried-and-tested classic. Traditional metal braces are highly effective for correcting all types of dental misalignments — from mild crowding to complex bite issues — at an accessible price point.

  • Traditional stainless steel brackets
  • Effective for all malocclusion types
  • Customizable with colored bands
  • Most economical orthodontic option
  • Average treatment: 18–24 months
📅 Book Now
💎
Orthodontics

Ceramic Braces

The aesthetic alternative to metal braces. Ceramic braces use tooth-colored or clear brackets that blend naturally with your teeth, making them much less visible while delivering the same effective results.

  • Tooth-colored ceramic brackets
  • Less noticeable than metal braces
  • Same efficacy as traditional braces
  • Ideal for adults & image-conscious teens
  • Stain-resistant modern materials
📅 Book Now
🕵️
Orthodontics

Lingual / Hidden Braces

Completely invisible orthodontic treatment! Lingual braces are bonded to the back (lingual) side of your teeth, making them 100% hidden from view — perfect for professionals and those who want a truly discreet option.

  • Completely invisible when smiling
  • Custom-fabricated for each patient
  • Works as effectively as front-facing braces
  • Ideal for professional adults
  • Advanced technology and materials
📅 Book Now
⚙️
Orthodontics

Self-Ligating Braces

Advanced orthodontic technology that uses a sliding mechanism instead of elastic bands — reducing friction, speeding up treatment time, and requiring fewer adjustments. Smaller, more comfortable, and highly efficient.

  • No elastic bands needed
  • Reduced friction for faster movement
  • Fewer clinic visits required
  • Smaller, more comfortable brackets
  • Better hygiene maintenance
📅 Book Now

Endodontics

Root Canal Treatment

Forget everything you've heard about root canals being painful! Our advanced rotary endodontic techniques and effective anaesthesia make the procedure completely comfortable — often performed in a single sitting.

  • Completely painless procedure
  • Often single-sitting treatment
  • Rotary endodontic system
  • Followed by custom crown placement
  • Saves your natural tooth
