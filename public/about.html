🏥 Our Story

Born from Passion, Built for Jaipur

LOL Dental by Dr. Sparsh is more than a dental clinic — it's a commitment to bring world-class dental care to the heart of Jagatpura, Jaipur. The idea was simple: why should the people of his hometown have to travel far for quality dental care when Dr. Sparsh could bring it right to their doorstep?

Dr. Sparsh Sharma completed his Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) from the prestigious SDM College of Dental Sciences, Dharwad — affiliated with Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS), Karnataka. During his studies and subsequent training across Hubli, Dharwad, and Bangalore, he developed a deep passion for cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, and patient-centric care.

After graduation, Dr. Sparsh was appointed as Clinic Head at one of India's largest dental chains — a testament to his clinical skills and leadership. There, he managed high volumes of diverse cases, led a team of dental professionals, and refined his philosophy: dental care should be painless, hygienic, transparent, and accessible.

In 2023, he returned to his roots — Jaipur — and established LOL Dental at Jagatpura. The clinic's name reflects his approachable personality and his belief that dentistry shouldn't be feared. From day one, the clinic has maintained a 5X sterilization protocol, modern equipment, and a warm, welcoming environment.

Today, LOL Dental is recognized as one of Jagatpura's best multispeciality dental clinics, serving families, professionals, and children with equally expert care — all under one roof, with your friendly neighbourhood doctor.
